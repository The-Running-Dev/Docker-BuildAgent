## Statement

**One publisher at a time.** At most one release-pipeline run publishes at any moment, held by a single CI concurrency group spanning every publishing workflow, queued rather than cancelled. Owner: Release pipeline (CI configuration). Enforcement: `[instruction]` — no `concurrency:` key exists in `.github/workflows/release.yml` or `.github/workflows/release-tag.yml` today
